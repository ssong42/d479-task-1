Transportation

Find information about getting to Taniti and getting around the island.

✈

Getting to Taniti

Most visitors arrive by air. The small airport accommodates small jets and propeller planes. A small cruise ship docks in Yellow Leaf Bay one night each week.

🚌

Getting around Taniti

Public buses serve Taniti City from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. Private buses serve the rest of the island. Taxis, rental cars, and rental bikes are also available. Taniti City is fairly flat and walkable.
