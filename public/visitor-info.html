Interesting facts about Taniti

A small island with varied scenery

Taniti covers less than 500 square miles and includes sandy and rocky beaches, a safe harbor, tropical rainforest, mountains, and a small active volcano.

Local community

About 20,000 indigenous people live on Taniti. Fishing and agriculture were important parts of the economy before tourism grew.

Visitor essentials

Food and restaurants

Ten restaurants offer local fish and rice, American-style meals, and Pan-Asian cuisine.

See restaurant options

Money and power

Taniti uses U.S. dollars. Many businesses accept euros, yen, and major credit cards. Power outlets are 120 volts.

Language

Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English. English is less common in rural areas, especially among older residents.

Health and safety

Taniti has one hospital, several clinics, and multilingual hospital employees. Violent crime is rare, but visitors should watch for petty theft.

Alcohol and closures

Alcohol cannot be sold or served from midnight to 9 a.m. Attractions and restaurants may close during national holidays.
