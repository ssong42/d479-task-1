Nature

Explore Taniti’s varied natural scenery, from its beaches to its mountainous interior.

Nature typeDescription
BeachesTaniti has sandy and rocky beaches. White sandy beaches encircle Yellow Leaf Bay near Taniti City.
RainforestVisitors can hike through the lush tropical rainforest or try zip-lining.
MountainsThe island’s interior is mountainous and offers a striking contrast to the coast.
VolcanoA small, active volcano is located in Taniti’s mountainous interior.
